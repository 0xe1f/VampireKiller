# Vampire Killer

Simon Belmont, a whip, and a castle that does not scroll. Every room is one screen. Walk off an edge, climb a stair, or drop — and if there is no floor under that drop, you die.

This is a player handbook for the MSX2 original. It is not a room-by-room walkthrough. Maps show everything: candles, chests, breakable walls, vendors, and who lives there.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Joystick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | Left / right | Stick |
| Crouch | Down (hold) | Down |
| Jump | Up (tap) | Trigger 2 |
| Whip / throw | Space | Trigger 1 |
| Pause | F1 | — |
| Map | F2 (after you find a [map]({{ '/manual/items/#map' | relative_url }})) | — |

You cannot walk while crouched. You *can* whip on the stairs.

Holy water and the hourglass are not the Space bar. While you are in the air: tap left or right for [holy water]({{ '/manual/weapons/#holy-water' | relative_url }}), tap down for the [hourglass]({{ '/manual/items/#hourglass' | relative_url }}). Each costs five hearts.

## The bar at the top

Score, stage number, hearts, lives (you start with three), your life, the enemy/boss life, the weapon you are holding, and lamps for the keys you have found.

Hearts are **money**, not health. They cap at 99. They buy from [vendors]({{ '/manual/vendors/' | relative_url }}), and they pay for holy water and the hourglass.

Your life bar is 32 points. In this handbook, damage and healing are written as a percent of that full bar — a small [life orb]({{ '/manual/items/#small-orb' | relative_url }}) is +25%, a [potion]({{ '/manual/items/#potion' | relative_url }}) is a full refill.

## How a stage works

A stage is a handful of rooms. Find the [white key]({{ '/manual/items/#white-key' | relative_url }}) (usually behind a wall or an awkward jump), walk to the door, leave. The courtyard is the exception: that door does not ask for a key.

The [yellow key]({{ '/manual/items/#yellow-key' | relative_url }}) opens one [chest]({{ '/manual/items/#chest' | relative_url }}). A [lockpick]({{ '/manual/items/#lockpick' | relative_url }}) opens three. You cannot hold both.

Whip candles. Whip suspicious brick. Some walls are fake; they look like the rest of the masonry until they aren't. The [maps]({{ '/manual/hubs/' | relative_url }}) in this handbook paint those blocks in gold so you do not have to guess.

Every third stage ends in a boss. Grab the orb when it falls if you want your life filled before the next block.

Death puts you back on leather, strips boots, wings, holy water, and the tipped-hourglass trick, and keeps the map if you had one.
