# Bestiary

Hit points are how many leather ticks drop fodder (chain and most throws do two). Contact is percent of Simon's full bar, unshielded. Score is for the kill.

Bosses use the long enemy bar. Leather vs chain percentages for that bar are on the [weapons]({{ '/manual/weapons/' | relative_url }}) page.
