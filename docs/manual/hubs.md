# The castle

Courtyard, then six blocks of three stages. Each block shares a look and a tune, and ends in a boss.

Maps, item lists, and who lives where: the stage pages. Common advice (find a white key, open the door) is on the [front page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}) once.

White outlines on the maps are breakable walls. Thin white boxes on stages 5 and 10 are moving platforms — the sprite is where the deck starts, the box is the rest of the path. Red is the white-key door. Teal pads (stage 12) are warps — the number is the destination room. Candles show their contents; chests show theirs. The black plate above a vendor is what he sells and the heart price with no bible. A boss stands in the room where the fight starts.
