# Stage 12 — Frankenstein and Igor

No up/down exits. Crouch on a floor pad, then tap up, to warp to its twin. The pairs are 0↔3, 1↔4, 2↔11, 5↔8, 7↔10.

Shortest run:

1. Walk one room left from the start. Take that pad, not the pad in the start room.
2. You land on its twin — do not take it back. Climb the left ledge, walk one room left, take the pad in that room.
3. Same again: leave the pad you landed on, walk one room left, take that room's pad.
4. White key; the boss is through the door on the right.

Igor starts on Frankenstein's head, then jumps.
