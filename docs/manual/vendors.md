# Vendors

<img src="{{ '/manual/assets/images/vendor.png' | relative_url }}" alt="A cloaked vendor">

He sits in a wall. Whip the masonry; the cloak is the tell. He is not an enemy. You cannot kill him.

Whip him again and the cloak flickers. Sometimes he tosses you **five hearts**. Sometimes he **takes five**. Sometimes nothing happens. Sometimes he puts an item in the air with a price. Sometimes he stands up and leaves — that last one is worth **5,000** points, and then he is gone.

It is a coin flip, run to run. There is no reliable "whip three times" recipe.

## Buying

While an offer is up:

- **Space** (or trigger) buys, if you have the hearts.
- **Shift** refuses.
- Wait too long and he withdraws the offer.

Prices below are in hearts. A [white bible]({{ '/manual/items/#white-bible' | relative_url }}) halves them (not always cleanly — the table is a little crooked). A [black bible]({{ '/manual/items/#black-bible' | relative_url }}) doubles them. You cannot hold both bibles.
