# Weapons

Space is the whip until you pick up a thrown weapon. Holy water does **not** replace Space — it is an extra throw while you jump. Death, or missing a boomerang catch, puts you back on leather.

Percents against bosses are of the **enemy bar** (128 points). Ordinary enemies have no bar; they die in a few connected hits.

## Leather whip {#leather-whip}

What you start with. One hit of fodder HP per connect. Against a boss bar, the light row: **3%** Dracula, **6%** Giant Bat / Medusa, **3%** Mummies / Frankenstein / Reaper.

## Chain whip {#chain-whip}

The upgrade. Two fodder HP per connect. Boss bar is about 1.5× leather: **5%** Dracula, **9%** Giant Bat / Medusa, **5%** Mummies / Frankenstein / Reaper. Space is still the whip.

## Knife {#knife}

Straight throw, no return, unlimited. A vendor sells it for **50** hearts (30 / 90 with the bibles). Hits despawn that knife. Two can be in the air. Damage like leather on bosses; **1** fodder HP per connect. Does not spend hearts to throw.

## Axe {#axe}

A world drop — vendors do not stock it. Slow boomerang. Catch it or you are back on leather. **4** fodder HP per connect; boss damage matches the chain. Short throw compared to the cross.

## Cross {#cross}

Faster boomerang, longer reach. A vendor sells it for **20** hearts (10 / 80). **2** fodder HP per connect; boss damage matches the chain. Miss the catch and you lose it.

## Holy water {#holy-water}

Jump, tap left or right (not a new Space). **Five hearts**, one vial at a time. It arcs, lands, and burns for a moment. **2** fodder HP per connect; the flame can hit more than once. Against a boss it uses whatever whip you have equipped, not a vial-specific number. Death loses the vial. Vendor price **30** (10 / 50).

<audio controls src="{{ '/manual/assets/audio/18_holy_water.wav' | relative_url }}"></audio>

Fodder (everything without a boss bar) in hits, leather vs chain/other: a 1-HP zombie dies to one leather tick; a 4-HP pikeman wants four leather or two chain. The axe is the impatient choice.
